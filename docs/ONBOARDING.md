# Onboarding a company

For the person at the platform who brings a new company on. About two hours of your time plus a one-hour training session. Follow it the same way for every company so nothing is forgotten.

## Before the first call
1. Confirm who the **company administrator** will be (one named person with authority to commit the company) and a second person who can step in.
2. Ask for: the company name, the administrator's work email, the number of staff and their roles, how many properties and tenancies there are, and whether the records are in spreadsheets (and in what shape).
3. Tell them honestly what exists today and what does not (see the release approval: no tax calculations, payments by bank transfer until online payments are proven).

## Setting up
1. The administrator registers the company at **Register a company**. They get a workspace and become Company Super Admin.
2. The administrator turns on **two-step sign-in** at *My account → Sign-in security* and stores the recovery codes. (Required for platform administrators; strongly recommended for company administrators.)
3. **Settings:** company profile and details.
4. **Team:** invite each staff member with the **least** role that lets them do their job:

| Role | Can | Cannot |
|---|---|---|
| Company Super Admin | Everything, including team, settings, billing, imports | |
| Manager | Assets, leases, record and reverse payments, approve adjustments, reports, listings | Team, settings, buying plans or advertising |
| Officer | Maintenance, inspections, inventory, documents, correspondence, deposits; can request adjustments | Record or reverse payments, approve adjustments, manage team |
| Agent | Listings, enquiries, clients, leads, deals | Finance |
| Viewer | Read-only on assets, finance reports and valuations | Change anything |

   Invitations work once, for the invited email, for 7 days. Until an email provider is connected the link is shown once on screen: send it to them directly.
5. **Bring in existing records** (`/app/import`) following `MIGRATION.md`: assets first, then leases with "Paid so far". Read the preview; confirm; read the reconciliation; save the report.
6. Spot-check three leases against the old spreadsheet: schedule, amount paid, amount owed.
7. Add the first documents (title, lease agreements) to a few assets.

## Training session (60 minutes, with the administrator and key staff)
Run it on their own data, not on a demo. Agenda:
1. (5 min) Sign in, two-step sign-in, the menu, where to get help (`/support`).
2. (10 min) An asset: its history, documents, parties.
3. (15 min) A lease: the rent schedule; record a payment with its evidence; what a receipt is; why a mistake is **reversed**, never edited; adjustments and approvals (two people).
4. (10 min) Arrears and the rent report; saving a report.
5. (10 min) Maintenance request to completed work order; inspections.
6. (5 min) Roles: what each person can and cannot do, and why.
7. (5 min) Questions. Leave the support contact and the day you will call them.

## After
- Day 2: call the administrator. Ask what confused anyone. Write it in the pilot feedback log.
- Week 1: check their activity (are payments being recorded? are people signing in?) and the ledger.
- Week 4: review with the administrator against the success measures in `PILOT.md`.
