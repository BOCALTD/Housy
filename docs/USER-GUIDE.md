# User guide

Short answers to "how do I…" for company staff. Each section names the menu item. Anything that changes money is recorded permanently: you correct a mistake with a new entry, you do not edit the old one.

## Sign in and stay safe
- Sign in with your email and password. If you turned on two-step sign-in, open "I use two-step sign-in" on the sign-in page and type the code from your authenticator app.
- Forgot your password: *Sign in → Forgot password*. The link works once for 60 minutes.
- *My account → Sign-in security*: change password (this signs out your other devices), turn two-step sign-in on or off, see recovery codes left, see your devices.
- Never tell anyone your password or code. Nobody at the platform will ask.

## Assets (properties and other things you own or manage)
- *Assets → Register asset*: name, class, category, how your company holds it (owner, manager, custodian, listing agent). The list shows 25 at a time; use search and Next/Previous.
- Open an asset to see its documents, parties, leases, history and map position.
- Upload documents (PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP or Word, up to 4 MB). Confidential letters are visible only to people allowed to see them.

## Leases and rent
- *Asset → New lease*: tenant, unit (blank means the whole property), dates, rent per period, how often. The system builds the full rent schedule.
- **Record a payment** on the lease: amount, date, and **evidence** (bank transfer reference, cheque number). The same evidence cannot be recorded twice. Payment is applied to the oldest unpaid rent first. You get a receipt number.
- **Wrong payment?** Reverse the receipt (a manager does this), then record the right one. The history shows both.
- **Late fees, waivers, discounts:** request an adjustment; a second person approves it.
- Renewals, notices, move-in and move-out handovers and deposits are on the lease page.
- **Send a payment link** (on the lease): the tenant pays online or by bank transfer through Housy. Paid online, the receipt is issued on the lease automatically.

## Country, currency and time zone
- People choose the country where they live when they join (*Profile and settings → Country and time zone* to change it). Their own plan is priced in its currency when Housy has set a price in it, and paid through the provider for that country.
- Dates and times are shown in your own time zone if you choose one there; otherwise in your workspace's (*Settings → Country and currency → Time zone*), otherwise your country's usual one. Quiet hours for emails follow your time zone. Times for inspecting a property are the time at the property.
- When a company or agent signs up, they choose the country where they do business. It sets the currency of their prices, payment links, plan and Housy's commission (₦, GH₵, KSh, R, $, £, C$, €, A$ or AED).
- *Settings → Country and currency* changes it, for a business that chose wrongly or moved. It is refused while a payment link is waiting or an invoice is unpaid, and the bank account for client payments must be added again.
- Listings show their own currency. When searching with a price range, choose the currency the range is in.

## Live chat
- When Housy's administrators switch it on, a Chat button sits at the bottom left (above the menu bar on phones). It starts only for people who accept optional cookies, because the chat provider sets its own.

## Client payments (company administrators)
- *Client payments*: add the bank account clients pay into, with the details your country's banks use (a NUBAN in Nigeria, a sort code in the UK, a routing number in the US, an IBAN in Europe and the UAE, a BSB in Australia).
- In Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and South Africa, online payments go through Paystack: choose the bank from the list (in Nigeria the bank confirms the account name) and payments settle to that account.
- In the US, UK, Canada, Europe, Australia and the UAE, online payments go through Stripe: choose *Set up card payments with Stripe*, give Stripe your business and bank details on its page, and online payments switch on once Stripe has checked them.
- Clients can always pay by bank transfer to the account shown on the payment page.
- **Inspection fee**: 0 keeps inspections free. With a fee, the person is asked to pay through Housy when you confirm the time; it is cancelled if the inspection is.
- **Ask for a payment** for a deposit, service charge or fee, and send the link. Mark it paid once a transfer arrives, with the bank reference.
- **Housy's commission** (a percentage set by Housy for rent, inspection fees and other payments) is taken automatically on online payments. On transfers you confirm and rent you record as received directly, it is invoiced on the first days of the next month under *Billing*.
- *Reports → Rent*: billed, collected and arrears for a period, 25 properties a page, saved as a spreadsheet with "Save or share".

## Maintenance, inspections, inventory, projects
- *Maintenance*: report a problem, assign a contractor, record work and costs, verify it is done.
- *Inspections*: templates, schedule, record findings with photographs, approve.
- *Inventory*: stock items, movements, counts.
- *Projects*: work packages, budgets, commitments, invoices.

## Compliance, correspondence and files
- *Compliance*: dated obligations (land use charge, insurance, permits). You enter the amounts and dates; the system reminds you. It does not calculate tax.
- *Correspondence*: register incoming and outgoing letters, assign, respond, mark confidential.
- *Files*: where the paper file is kept.

## Selling and renting out (agents)
- *Listings*: publish a property. A platform administrator reviews it first.
- *CRM*: clients, leads, deals, commissions.

## Your team and company (administrators)
- *Settings → Pause or close this workspace* (top administrator only). **Pause**: listings and profiles leave public view, the team cannot work in it, the plan is not renewed; resume it from the page shown when you open the workspace. **Close for good**: type CLOSE and your password; waiting payment links and the plan stop, records are kept, and only Housy support can reopen it. Each asks you to confirm first.
- For yourself: *Profile and settings → Take a break* hides your profile and pauses emails until you sign in again; *Privacy → Delete your account* removes it. Both ask you to confirm.
- *Team*: invite, change role, suspend. You cannot change your own role.
- *Settings*, *Billing*, *Advertising*.
- *Bring in records*: see `MIGRATION.md`.

## The assistant
It can find properties from a sentence, suggest document types, summarise letters and answer questions from a fixed list of reports. It only suggests; a person decides. It never writes valuation, legal or financial figures.

## Getting help
*Help and support* at the bottom of every page: contact details, and what to do when you cannot sign in or money looks wrong.
