# User guide

Short answers to "how do I…" for company staff. Each section names the menu item. Anything that changes money is recorded permanently: you correct a mistake with a new entry, you do not edit the old one.

## Sign in and stay safe
- Sign in with your email and password. If you turned on two-step sign-in, open "I use two-step sign-in" on the sign-in page and type the code from your authenticator app.
- Forgot your password: *Sign in → Forgot password*. The link works once for 60 minutes.
- *My account → Sign-in security*: change password (this signs out your other devices), turn two-step sign-in on or off, see recovery codes left, see your devices.
- Never tell anyone your password or code. Nobody at the platform will ask.

## Assets (properties and other things you own or manage)
- *Assets → Register asset*: name, class, category, how your company holds it (owner, manager, custodian, listing agent). The list shows 25 at a time; use search and Next/Previous.
- Open an asset to see its documents, parties, leases, history and map position.
- Upload documents (PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP or Word, up to 4 MB). Confidential letters are visible only to people allowed to see them.

## Leases and rent
- *Asset → New lease*: tenant, unit (blank means the whole property), dates, rent per period, how often. The system builds the full rent schedule.
- **Record a payment** on the lease: amount, date, and **evidence** (bank transfer reference, cheque number). The same evidence cannot be recorded twice. Payment is applied to the oldest unpaid rent first. You get a receipt number.
- **Wrong payment?** Reverse the receipt (a manager does this), then record the right one. The history shows both.
- **Late fees, waivers, discounts:** request an adjustment; a second person approves it.
- Renewals, notices, move-in and move-out handovers and deposits are on the lease page.
- *Reports → Rent*: billed, collected and arrears for a period, 25 properties a page, saved as a spreadsheet with "Save or share".

## Maintenance, inspections, inventory, projects
- *Maintenance*: report a problem, assign a contractor, record work and costs, verify it is done.
- *Inspections*: templates, schedule, record findings with photographs, approve.
- *Inventory*: stock items, movements, counts.
- *Projects*: work packages, budgets, commitments, invoices.

## Compliance, correspondence and files
- *Compliance*: dated obligations (land use charge, insurance, permits). You enter the amounts and dates; the system reminds you. It does not calculate tax.
- *Correspondence*: register incoming and outgoing letters, assign, respond, mark confidential.
- *Files*: where the paper file is kept.

## Selling and renting out (agents)
- *Listings*: publish a property. A platform administrator reviews it first.
- *CRM*: clients, leads, deals, commissions.

## Your team and company (administrators)
- *Team*: invite, change role, suspend. You cannot change your own role.
- *Settings*, *Billing*, *Advertising*.
- *Bring in records*: see `MIGRATION.md`.

## The assistant
It can find properties from a sentence, suggest document types, summarise letters and answer questions from a fixed list of reports. It only suggests; a person decides. It never writes valuation, legal or financial figures.

## Getting help
*Help and support* at the bottom of every page: contact details, and what to do when you cannot sign in or money looks wrong.
